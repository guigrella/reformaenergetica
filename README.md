Reforma Energética is a Brazilian educational initiative that combines architecture, Feng Shui, neuroarchitecture, and technology to help people transform their living and working environments, promoting well-being and quality of life.

Our mission is to democratize specialized knowledge that has traditionally been accessible primarily through individual consulting services, making it available through practical, accessible, and scalable educational programs.

**Current Reach and Impact**

- 3,356 students enrolled to date.
- Approximately 1,000 currently active students.
- An international presence across 11 countries.
- A lead database of approximately 25,000 potential students.
- A combined social media following of over 297,000:
  - Instagram: 269,000 followers.
  - TikTok: 23,400 followers.
  - YouTube: 4,730 subscribers.

Our educational ecosystem includes online courses, live workshops, professional training, personalized environmental assessments, and student communities.

The project is led by a multidisciplinary team:

- **Roberta Ruschel:** Architect and Urban Planner with postgraduate specialization in Neuroarchitecture, responsible for methodology, educational content, and specialized knowledge.
- **Guilherme Grella:** Co-founder and Computer Engineering graduate, responsible for technological development, digital infrastructure, and innovation.

**Our Vision for Technology**

Our goal is to evolve from a traditional online education business into a technology-driven knowledge and learning ecosystem.

We are developing and planning proprietary technological solutions to support both our internal operations and our students' learning experiences, including:

- **AI-powered educational assistants:** providing personalized guidance based on our proprietary methodology.
- **Intelligent knowledge management:** centralizing, transcribing, indexing, and structuring our educational content into an accessible and searchable knowledge base.
- **Personalized learning platforms:** adapting educational journeys to individual needs, challenges, and progress.
- **AI-assisted environmental assessments:** analyzing photographs, floor plans, and questionnaires to support personalized recommendations.
- **Proprietary CRM and communication infrastructure:** improving student relationships, customer support, engagement, and operational efficiency.
- **Internal automation and data intelligence:** integrating workflows, reducing dependency on fragmented third-party platforms, and enabling data-driven decisions.
- **Scalable technological infrastructure:** supporting the development, deployment, and continuous improvement of our applications and AI-powered services.

**The Gap We Fill**

There is a significant opportunity at the intersection of environmental well-being, architecture, education, and artificial intelligence.

Specialized knowledge in these fields is often fragmented, expensive, difficult to access, and dependent on individual professional availability.

We aim to bridge this gap by building an integrated ecosystem that transforms expert knowledge into personalized, accessible, and scalable educational experiences.

Unlike early-stage projects that are still searching for product-market fit, Reforma Energética already has an established educational methodology, a paying student base, international reach, and a substantial digital audience.

Technology is the next step in expanding our existing impact.

Our long-term vision is to build an international educational technology platform capable of serving thousands of students simultaneously, democratizing specialized knowledge while improving the quality, accessibility, and personalization of education through artificial intelligence.
